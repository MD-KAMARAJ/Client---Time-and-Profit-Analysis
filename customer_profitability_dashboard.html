How management should act

Retain

Profitable or strategically important clients with healthy renewal and manageable service load.

Reprice

Clients with good strategic value but weak margin because effort or delays are too high.

Optimize

Accounts where escalations, duration, or resources are inflating cost beyond revenue.

Exit

High-loss, low-renewal, high-churn clients with no realistic recovery path.

Page 4 converts analysis into action by combining profit, delays, escalations, renewal status, satisfaction, and churn risk to decide what should happen next for each client.
